## Understanding Squint Eye : Definition and Types

Squint eye, medically known as strabismus, is a condition where the eyes do not align properly.

Key Characteristics

* One eye looks straight ahead while the other:Turns inwardTurns outwardMoves upwardMoves downward
* Can be:Constant or intermittentAffect one or both eyes

* Turns inward
* Turns outward
* Moves upward
* Moves downward

* Affect one or both eyes

Why It Matters

* Not just a cosmetic issue
* Can affect:Vision clarityDepth perceptionOverall quality of life

* Vision clarity
* Depth perception
* Overall quality of life

Types of Strabismus

Direction:

* Esotropia– inward turning (“cross-eyed”)
* Exotropia– outward turning (“wall-eyed”)
* Hypertropia– upward deviation
* Hypotropia– downward deviation

Onset:

* Congenital– present at birth
* Acquired– develops later

Cause:

* Non-paralytic strabismus– due to muscle imbalance
* Paralytic strabismus– due to nerve damage
