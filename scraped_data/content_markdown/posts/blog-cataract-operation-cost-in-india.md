Cataract surgery is one of the most common and successful eye procedures performed worldwide. ## What Is Cataract Surgery?

A cataract is a clouding of the eye’s natural lens, leading to blurry vision and, if left untreated, potential blindness. Cataract surgery involves removing the cloudy lens and replacing it with a clear artificial intraocular lens (IOL). The procedure is typically quick—often completed in under 30 minutes—and has a high success rate

### Insurance & Government Support
