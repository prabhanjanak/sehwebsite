### Types of Eye Specialists Available in Varanasi

The availability of subspecialty-based ophthalmology makes it easier to find thebest eye specialist in Varanasiaccording to individual needs. Common specialties include:


##### Cataractand General Ophthalmology:

These specialists manage age-related cataracts, refractive errors, eye infections, dry eye, and routine vision problems. Cataract surgery today is safe and highly effective when performed by experienced hands.


##### Vitreo Retina Services:

Retina specialists treat complex conditions affecting the back of the eye, such as diabetic retinopathy, macular degeneration and retinal detachment. Timely retinal care plays a crucial role in preventing permanent vision loss.


##### Glaucoma Services:

Glaucoma is known as the “silent thief of vision” because it often has no early symptoms. Aglaucoma specialistfocuses on early diagnosis, pressure control, and long-term monitoring to preserve vision.


##### Paediatric Ophthalmology and Squint Care:

Children require specialized eye care for conditions like squint, lazy eye, congenital cataracts, and refractive errors. Early treatment ensures proper visual development and prevents lifelong vision problems.


##### Orbit and Oculoplasty Services:

These specialists treat structural, functional, and cosmetic conditions of the eye and surrounding tissues, including eyelid disorders, trauma, and reconstructive procedures.
