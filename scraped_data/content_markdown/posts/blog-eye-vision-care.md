## Eye Vision care

Despite being genuinely remarkable organs, your eyes are subject to stress & injury and are vulnerable to damage. In most cases, people do not have a problem with their vision until they are directed to get a pair of glasses or contact lenses as their sight has started deteriorating. No matter your age, maintaining the health of your eyes is essential by taking proper eye vision care . A clear vision will improve your ability to learn, comprehend, and stay healthy, thus resulting in a better quality of life.

A regular vision care examination with theBest eye specialist in Indiais also essential to keeping your eyes healthy. Examinations include tests for visual acuity, alignment of your eyes, eye movement, and depth perception.

Added advantage of getting your vision care checked regularly is that while your doctor may use eye drops to dilate the pupils, they may even spot conditions like high blood pressure or diabetes before your primary care provider does.

Change your life with vision care

Manycommon eye diseasescan cause permanent vision loss or blindness if not treated early. Some of them are:


### 1. Glaucoma– Optic nerve damage caused by different conditions


### 2. Macular degeneration – Progressive loss of cells that are sensitive to light


### 3.Cataracts– Cloudy vision


### 4. Diabetic retinopathy– damages blood vessels behind the eye

Toprevent the eye problems, here are some measures you should follow :

Eat a balanced diet – A balanced diet should contain a wide variety of fruits and vegetables, preferably green leafy vegetables. Salmon, tuna, and halibut are also good sources of omega-3 fatty acids that may benefit your eyes.

Maintain a healthy weight – Being overweight or obese can put you at risk of developing diabetes, and nursing a diabetes diagnosis exposes you to diabetic retinopathy and glaucoma.

Regular exercise regimen maintain- Regular exercise can help you avoid conditions like diabetes, hypertension, and hypercholesterolemia. As a result, physical activity can lower your chances of developing these eye and vision problems.

Put on protective eyewear – Whether you’re playing sports, working in a factory, or performing home repairs, eye protection is vital.

Stop smoking – Reducing or quitting smoking can help you avoid age-related eye conditions like macular degeneration and cataracts.

Understand your family’s medical history – Some eye conditions are hereditary, so keeping track of your family’s health history is imperative. By doing so, you can figure out if you are vulnerable to eye diseases.

Rest youreyes– Spending excessive time in front of a computer can strain your eyes. To reduce eye fatigue, follow the 20-20-20 rule: Every 20 minutes, look 20 feet in front of you for 20 seconds.
