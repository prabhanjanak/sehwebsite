## Beginnings


## Beginnings

In May 1977, drawing inspiration from the Sankaracharyas of Kanchi, Dr. R V Ramani and Dr. Radha Ramani established a Primary Health Care Centre in Coimbatore to address the needs of the underprivileged. Through the collaboration of esteemed eye care professionals offering their expertise voluntarily and the unwavering support of Coimbatore’s local community, this humble initiative blossomed into a network of 14 Medical Centers spanning 10 states. Today, these centers collectively serve over 1000 patients daily, embodying the spirit of compassionate care and community service.

## Founder’s Message


## Founder’s Message

In 1985, Dr.R V Ramani became acutely aware of the transformative impact that the “Gift of Vision” could bestow. Recognizing that over 80% of blindness is preventable and treatable, he envisioned Sankara as a beacon of hope in the fight against needless blindness, operating under the auspices of the Sri Kanchi Kamakoti Medical Trust.

Aligned with Mahatma Gandhi’s profound assertion that “India lives in the villages,” Dr. R.V. Ramani introduced the Gift of Vision initiative in 1990. This groundbreaking program aimed to deliver high-quality, affordable eye care directly to rural India’s doorstep. Through the collaborative efforts of Rotary International and Rotary Coimbatore Central, the Gift of Vision program was launched, marking a significant milestone in India’s healthcare landscape.

The program’s model is built on the principle of providing world-class eye care services free of charge to 80% of the population, while leveraging paid services for the remaining 20%. This innovative approach ensures that the cost of treatment is subsidized, making quality eye care accessible to all, regardless of economic status.

## National Replication


## National Replication

Replicating the sustainable Model was the next big dream of Dr. R V Ramani. In 2002 a grand vision of replicating the sustainable eye care model across the country was envisaged.

His relentless pursuit towards enabling sight for the less-fortunate brought International support: Sankara Eye Foundation established in USA became an integral part of the Sankara’s vision for National replication and that led to the first replication outside Coimbatore in Guntur, Andhra Pradesh.

Today with the strong commitment towards eradicating curable blindness 14 Super Speciality Sankara Eye Hospitals have been set up across 10 states of the country.

The collaborative efforts of our team of Doctors, Administrators, Staff, Donors, and Supporters have undoubtedly been instrumental in shaping the success of Sankara. Their active engagement and dedication has played a crucial role in achieving the noble mission of restoring sight.
