## What is Contoura Vision LASIK

Contoura Vision LASIK is a topography-guided laser procedure that goes beyond standard LASIK by correcting corneal irregularities and improving overall visual quality. Designed for precision and safety, it provides sharper vision and reduces visual distortions like glare and halos.

Contoura Vision is an advanced, US-FDA-approved topography-guided laser eye surgery designed to correct refractive errors (nearsightedness, farsightedness, and astigmatism) and reduce or eliminate the need for glasses or contact lenses. It maps 22,000 unique points on the cornea to treat individual surface irregularities.

### How It Works:

Mapping:Scans 22,000 distinct points on the cornea’s surface to detect microscopic texture and shape flaws.Axis Alignment:Unlike standard LASIK,which aligns with the center of the pupil, Contoura treats along the natural visual axis.Procedure:A thin corneal flap is created, and an excimer laser precisely reshapes the underlying tissue in a matter of seconds.


### Key Benefits

Sharper Sight:Frequently yields sharper visual acuity than standard glasses, contacts, or traditional LASIK.Fewer Side Effects:Significantly reduces night-vision issues like glare, halos, and starbursts.Fast Recovery:The outpatient procedure takes about 15 minutes for both eyes, is virtually painless, and allows for rapid healing.
