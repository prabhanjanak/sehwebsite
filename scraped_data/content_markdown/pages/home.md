# From Patient Testimonies, to World Class Eye Care

Visioned towards building a sustainable eye care model across the country,Sankara was started with the mission to work towards eradicating needless blindness.

Sankara Eye Foundation India, a not-for-profit entity founded by Dr. R.V. Ramani and Dr. Radha Ramani in 1977, is dedicated to delivering high-quality eye care services to underserved communities. What began as a modest primary health care center has blossomed into a formidable social enterprise. Presently, Sankara stands as a beacon of hope, managing a network of 14 cutting-edge super specialty eye care hospitals strategically positioned across Tamil Nadu, Andhra Pradesh, Karnataka, Gujarat, Punjab, Uttar Pradesh, Madhya Pradesh, Rajasthan, Maharashtra, and Telangana.

## Social Impact

Sankara is committed to bring the positive changes in the eye care sector by addressing the challenges of non-availability and non-affordability of quality eye care. The global burden of blindness due to cataract is increasing day by day. In such scenario Sankara‘s goal is to eliminate needless blindness.


### Maitri

Vision rehabilitation cannot restore lost sight, but it can equip you with techniques to maintain an independent lifestyle.


### Vision Centres

Provide specialized, technologically advanced and affordable eye care services in areas where specialist doctors may not be easily accessible.


### Women’s Empowerment

Directed towards empowering young women from poor socio-economic sections of society and developing HR to meet the eye care needs of country.


### Diabetic Retinopathy

Delivering cost effective ophthalmic care at the appropriate time to reduce the suffering caused by blindness and vision impairment due to Diabetic Retinopathy.


### Nannu Kannu

Successfully implementing a Comprehensive Paediatric Eye Care Program for visually handicapped, underprivileged school children of Karnataka.


### Children’s Eye Care (Rainbow)

Focused in providing a bright future to visually handicapped children by increasing early detection of eye diseases to prevent visual impairment.
