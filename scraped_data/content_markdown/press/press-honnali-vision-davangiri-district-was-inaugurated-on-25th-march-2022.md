# Honnali Vision – Davangiri District was inaugurated on 25th March 2022.

“ Sankaras’ 30th Vision Centre at Honnali- Davangiri District was inaugurated on 25th March 2022. The Vision Centre was inaugurated by Dr. Murlidhar – District Program Manager of Davangiri Dist and offered in the service of community. To grace the occasion, Dr. Mahesh S. – C.M.O., Ms. Gayathri – unit Head and Ms. Anitha – Sr. Administrator of Sankara Eye Hospital, Shimoga were present with their staff members”
