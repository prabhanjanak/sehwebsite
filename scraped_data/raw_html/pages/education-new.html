DNB in Ophthalmology

REQUIREMENTS:

Primary (post MBBS) DNB. Must have passed MBBS, completed 1 yr rotatory internship training and must have registered with Medical council of India or its state branches. Duration is 3 years. Should have passed CET conducted by the NBE.

LOCATION AND SEAT MATRIX:

Tamil Nadu, Coimbatore – 5 post MBBS and 5 post DO seats.
Andhra Pradesh, Guntur – 4 post MBBS and 4 post DO seats.
Karnataka, Shimoga – 3 post MBBS and 3 post DO seats.
Karnataka, Bangalore – 3 post MBBS and 3 post DO seats.
Uttar Pradesh, Kanpur – 2 post MBBS and 2 post DO seats

 

SELECTION:
Through CET/ PDCET merit cum choice based centralized counselling by  NBE, New Delhi Please visit NBE website www.natboard.edu.in  for more and updated information.

STIPEND: As per NBE guidelines.

AWARD OF DIPLOMA:

On successful completion of course, acceptance of thesis and passing of final theory and practical examination conducted by the NBE, it confirms award of diploma, DNB.

PEDAGOGY:

Primary outcome measure of DNB programme at SECI is proficiency in diagnosis and management of eye diseases of public health importance and the common eye problems. The secondary measure is exposure to diagnosis and management of eyes related to various subspecialties, rare eye diseases and relevant systemic diseases. Additional outcome measure is participation in research, publication and presentations.

The knowledge and skill necessary to achieve the above measures, is imparted through
1) Posting in OPD, ward, OT, refraction unit, orthoptics and binocular vision units, lab and peripheral services;
2) Hands-on in clinical work-up, diagnostics, lab investigations and wet lab;
3) Participation in didactic lectures, seminars, interdisciplinary meetings, journal clubs and case presentations;
4) Participation in research, publication, professional meetings, workshops, conferences and CMEs.

The learning is facilitated through individual attention, flexible schedule, library, internet and everything in a clean, green, natural and serene atmosphere.

Sankara Geuder Competence Centre (SGCC)

Though nearly 900 ophthalmologists trained in India every year, it is estimated that India needs 25,000 ophthalmologists by 2020 to achieve goals of VISION 2020: The Right to Sight. Apart from increasing the number of fresh ophthalmic surgeons, it is imperative that the productivity of practicing ophthalmologists be increased significantly so that they function more efficiently and effectively.

A unique partnership between a leading European Ophthalmic Equipment manufacturer Geuder, AG of Heidelberg, Germany and SAV has helped create an infrastructure that would provide training for ophthalmologists in Cataract Surgery and VR techniques under supervision. It would also provide for training of Biomedical Engineering field staff and paramedical staff of users in a clinical environment.

CURRENT TRAINING OFFERED:

Phacoemulsification Short term Training (Ophthalmologists)

This is an on-going programme with over 40 surgeons trained from as far as Bangalore, Patna, Chennai, Hubli, Bagalkote, Koppal, Nammakal, Mysore, Calicut, Bijapur, Mangalore, Kolar and Rajasthan. The training is offered in 2 modules the basic course for 1 month, requires the surgeon to be proficient in tunneling, rhexis and hydro procedures. The surgeons should have a minimum experience 300 MSICS and a Post-graduation Qualification recognized by MCI.

An advanced course of 15 Days is offered to surgeons familiar with phacoemulsification who wish to hone their techniques or learn more modern concepts in phacoemulsification surgery.

For details of training write to [email protected]


Vitreoretina Training (Ophthalmologists)

The department offers a surgical retina fellowship in Vitreoretina and ocular oncology affiliated to the Rajiv Gandhi University of Health Sciences, Karnataka. There are 2 intakes – July and December.

Leveraging on the expertise of our faculty and the large variety of cases handled at our hospital, we offer observer ships to vitreoretinal surgeons from India and overseas.
